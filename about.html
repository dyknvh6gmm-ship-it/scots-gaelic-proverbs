Why this site exists

Sean-fhaclan & Auld Sayins started as a way to search Scots and Scottish Gaelic proverbs side by side and see where their wisdom overlaps — and where each language has sayings the other doesn't. It's a starter set, built to grow: proverbs, translations, and even a proverb's place of origin can all be added over time, with sources credited honestly rather than guessed at.
